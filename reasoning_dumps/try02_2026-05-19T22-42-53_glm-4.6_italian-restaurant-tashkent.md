While taste is subjective, **Cotto Piccolo** is widely considered the best Italian restaurant in Tashkent by locals, expats, and food critics alike.

Here is a breakdown of the top Italian spots in the city depending on what you are looking for:

### 1. The Overall Best: **Cotto Piccolo**
This is part of the famous *Creative Group* (known for the best fine dining in Tashkent).
*   **Why it’s the best:** They have a wood-fired brick oven imported from Italy, which produces authentic, Neapolitan-style pizza with a perfect leopard-spotted crust. Their pasta is made fresh daily, and the ingredients are often imported.
*   **Best for:** Pizza, authentic pasta, and a stylish, rustic atmosphere.
*   **Location:** Amir Temur Avenue.

### 2. Best for Fine Dining & Steak: **Parmigiano**
Also owned by the Creative Group, this is the slightly more elegant "sister" restaurant to Cotto Piccolo.
*   **The Vibe:** It feels more upscale and "white tablecloth." The interior is designed to look like a luxurious Italian mansion.
*   **Best for:** A romantic dinner, business lunches, and excellent meat/fish dishes (though their pasta is also top-tier).
*   **Location:** Gulyamov Street (near the Japanese Garden).

### 3. Best Modern Bistro Vibe: **Amico Bistro**
If you want something less formal than Parmigiano but quieter than a bustling pizzeria, this is the place.
*   **The Vibe:** Modern, trendy, and comfortable. It bridges the gap between a restaurant and a casual hang-out spot.
*   **Best for:** Truffle dishes, risotto, and a relaxed dinner with cocktails.
*   **Location:** Osiyo Street.

### 4. The "Cozy" Favorite: **Mia Famiglia**
This is a long-standing local favorite. It might not have the ultra-modern design of the Creative Group restaurants, but it has a very loyal following.
*   **The Vibe:** Very cozy, warm, and family-friendly.
*   **Best for:** Classic comfort food, large portions, and a casual vibe.

### Summary Recommendation
*   If you want the **absolute best Pizza/Pasta**: Go to **Cotto Piccolo**.
*   If you want a **fancy date night**: Go to **Parmigiano**.
